What this database does — and does not — prove. GLEIF contains French legal entities that have an LEI; it is not a complete list of every small business or employer. An active entity status and an issued LEI are useful identity checks, but they do not prove that a company is hiring, that a website belongs to it, or that a job offer is genuine. GullyHire keeps every result as a candidate until its official domain and careers source are independently checked.
